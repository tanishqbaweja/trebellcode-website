Privacy policy

Plain-language
website privacy.

This policy covers the public Trebell websites at trebell.dev and code.trebell.dev. It does not pretend a static marketing site has a giant data pipeline when it does not.

Effective: October 8, 2026

What the website collects

The current public marketing site does not include analytics, advertising trackers, account registration, or contact forms. The site's theme controls may store a theme preference in your browser's local storage. That preference stays in your browser unless your browser or extensions synchronize it.

Infrastructure requests

The websites are served using third-party hosting and network infrastructure. Those providers may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, request path, timestamps, and security-related information as necessary to deliver and protect the site. Some pages also request web fonts from Google Fonts, which may expose normal request metadata to that provider.

Email

If you email founder@trebell.dev, the information you include is processed for the purpose of responding to your message, investigating an issue, or continuing the business conversation you initiated.

Trebell Code and model providers

Trebell Code is separate software. When you configure the product to use a third-party model provider or service, data sent to that provider is subject to the provider's own terms and privacy practices. Visiting this website by itself does not send your local repository to Trebell.

Cookies

The current site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Infrastructure providers may use their own technical mechanisms when required for security or delivery.

Changes

As Trebell adds hosted services, accounts, analytics, or commercial team infrastructure, this policy will be updated before those changes are represented as current practice.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to founder@trebell.dev.
